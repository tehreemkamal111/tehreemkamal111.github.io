**AI Researcher · Stream Data Analytics & Machine Learning Lab, Novosibirsk State University · June 2026–present**

## Research focus

Developing and validating deep learning pipelines for white matter and gray matter segmentation in brain MRI of multiple sclerosis patients, with attention to MS lesions and anatomical variations.

## My contribution

- Develop medical image segmentation pipelines using PyTorch, nnU-Net, and MONAI.
- Prepare MRI data through preprocessing, normalization, and augmentation.
- Evaluate results using Dice score, Hausdorff distance, and related medical image analysis metrics.
- Collaborate on MRI annotation, result validation, and interpretation.

**Tools:** Python · PyTorch · nnU-Net · MONAI
