**Final year project · Dawood University of Engineering and Technology · 2022**

## The project

_Automated Industrial Inspection System for Plastic Bottle Defects_ combines computer vision and a physical imaging setup to detect bottle defects in real time.

## My contribution

I developed an AI inspection system using **TensorFlow Lite** for **dent and crack detection** in plastic bottles. The system integrates a custom experimental testbed and a servo motor controlled imaging setup.

The project received **National Technology Fund (Ignite) funding in 2022**.

## Related engineering work

As an AI Engineer at the Neurocomputational Lab, NCAI, NED University of Engineering and Technology, I also worked on TensorFlow models for bottle defect detection. That role included dataset construction and annotation, hyperparameter tuning, validation, and Django and Flask applications for model deployment.
