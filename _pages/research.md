## Brain MRI segmentation

At the **Stream Data Analytics & Machine Learning Lab, Novosibirsk State University**, I work on deep learning methods for segmenting **white matter and gray matter** in brain MRI of patients with **multiple sclerosis**.

MS lesions and anatomical variations make tissue segmentation challenging. My research focuses on developing and validating pipelines that account for these differences.

### Research workflow

- **Prepare the data:** MRI preprocessing, image normalization, and augmentation.
- **Develop the models:** segmentation pipelines using PyTorch, nnU-Net, and MONAI.
- **Evaluate the results:** Dice score, Hausdorff distance, and related segmentation metrics.
- **Validate the analysis:** collaborate on MRI annotation, result validation, and interpretation.

## Computer vision for industrial inspection

My final year project at Dawood University of Engineering and Technology explored **automated plastic bottle inspection**. I developed a deep learning system with TensorFlow Lite for real-time dent and crack detection, integrating a custom experimental testbed and a servo motor controlled imaging setup.

The project received **Ignite funding in 2022**. I continued working on bottle defect detection, dataset annotation, and model deployment as an AI Engineer at the Neurocomputational Lab, NCAI, NED University of Engineering and Technology.
