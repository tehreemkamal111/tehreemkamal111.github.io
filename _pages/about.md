I am an **AI researcher and software developer**, currently pursuing an MS in Artificial Intelligence & Big Data Analytics at **Novosibirsk State University**, Russia.

At the university's **Stream Data Analytics & Machine Learning Lab**, I work on deep learning for white matter and gray matter segmentation in brain MRI of multiple sclerosis patients. My work brings together medical image preprocessing, segmentation pipelines, and evaluation using **PyTorch, nnU-Net, and MONAI**.

Alongside research, I develop responsive web applications at **MINIME-LABS**, where I contribute to the frontends of **Ezehome** and **Sentry**. My background includes AI engineering, computer vision, and frontend and backend development.

I earned my BE in Computer System Engineering from **Dawood University of Engineering and Technology**, Pakistan. My final year project, an automated inspection system for plastic bottle defects, received **Ignite funding in 2022**.
